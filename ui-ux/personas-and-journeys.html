Day 1 Deliverable — UI/UX · AI-assisted SME loan origination

Aitbaar sits on one loan file with three people around it: the SME owner who applies, the loan officer who decides, and the Head of SME Banking who has to trust the system at portfolio scale. The two primary users sit on opposite sides of the same file — every design decision is judged by whether it removes friction for both at once.
MI
Persona 1 · Applicant · Primary User
Muhammad Imran
41 · Shah Alam Market, Lahore
Wholesale/retail cloth · 6 yrs · 3 employees
Android · WhatsApp open all day
Urdu-first, limited written English
💰 ~PKR 850,000/mo sales — cash + JazzCash
Snapshot — Real, profitable business; never taken a formal loan. Keeps a hand-written ledger (bahi). No laptop, printer, or scanner. Wants working capital before Eid / wedding season. The bank feels like a closed door.
Goals
  • Fast working capital before the season passes
  • No repeated branch trips
  • Judged on his business, not on contacts
  • Know exactly what's needed and where he stands
Frustrations
  • 3–5 branch trips, each for one more undeclared document
  • Weeks-long decisions, unexplained rejection
  • Feels it runs on sifarish, not merit
  • English forms are a barrier
Behaviours
  • Already sends document photos over WhatsApp; won't install a new app for one loan
  • Falls back to committee / family / aarti when refused by a bank
"Har baar naya kaghaz maang lete hain. Mujhe pehle hi bata dein kya chahiye, aur wajah bata dein agar mana karna hai."
How Aitbaar helps
  • Applies from his own counter, on WhatsApp, in Urdu
  • Full document checklist declared up front, one-time consent
  • Status pushed at every step — zero branch visits to apply
  • If something's missing, told the one exact item — nothing vague

ST
Persona 2 · Loan Officer · Primary User
Sana Tariq
33 · SME Credit / Relationship Officer, UBL Branch · 8 years experience
💻 Branch desktop, bank email, internal systems
📄 40–60 page files, several open at once
Snapshot — Wants to hit SME targets but is the bottleneck. Reads every file by hand, cross-checks herself, writes decisions under time pressure. Cost to assess a small file is close to a large file, so small tickets get pushed aside.
Goals
  • Process more files a day without cutting corners
  • Consistent, defensible decisions
  • Spend time on judgement, not data entry
  • Catch fraud before it becomes a bad loan
Frustrations
  • Manual extraction and verification by eye
  • Two officers score the same file differently
  • No structured record — decisions are hard to audit
  • Chasing missing docs one at a time; small tickets not worth the hours
"I do not need the machine to decide for me. I need it to read the file, flag the risks, and let me approve in minutes instead of hours."
How Aitbaar helps
  • One-page credit brief instead of 50 pages — score /100, risk tier, top factors, fraud flags, recommended range
  • One click through to any source document
  • Approve, decline, or request exactly one missing item
  • Every decision + reasoning lands in an audit trail
AQ
Persona 3 · Economic Buyer
Adnan Qureshi
Head of SME Banking, UBL
Portfolio growth Turnaround time Risk Cost per file Regulatory defensibility
Snapshot — Under pressure to grow the SME book toward the SBP target of 750,000 borrowers by 2028. Can't hire his way to the throughput; needs technology that raises officer capacity without raising risk — and that a regulator accepts.
Goals
  • More new SME borrowers at a controlled default rate
  • Turnaround cut from weeks to days
  • Higher files-per-officer-per-day without headcount
  • Only explainable, auditable, consent-based decisioning
How Aitbaar helps
  • Weeks-long manual file → same-day, explainable decision at ~PKR 30/assessment
  • Runs on a channel (WhatsApp) and core system (Temenos) the bank already uses
  • AI recommends, officer decides — every decision auditable
  • A direct answer to the SBP's call for tech + data + fintech partnerships
